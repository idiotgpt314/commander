# Monetization Without Ads

Commander stays free and usable without ads.

## Good Revenue Options

- GitHub Sponsors for individual supporters
- Open Collective for transparent community funding
- paid setup/support for teams that want distro rollout and policy tuning
- paid "pro" services around deployment, fleet rollout, and enterprise support
- optional cloud relay or sync services later, while the local app remains free

## What To Avoid

- ads in the launcher
- paywalling the core summon flow
- gating bug fixes behind payment
- adding upsell clutter to the prompt surface

## Revenue Sequence

1. free product with tight packaging and good docs
2. donation/support links in GitHub and docs
3. paid support and setup offers
4. optional hosted collaboration features later if they do not weaken local-first use
